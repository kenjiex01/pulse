## 16:03 — Use Payroll Calendar Days on employee salary

**Why:** Semi-monthly basic pay should keep Days Per Period at 26 for the salary-screen hourly rate, while each payroll cutoff uses that calendar's working days.

**Summary:**
- New salary checkbox: **Use Payroll Calendar Days**.
- Salary setup Hourly Rate = Basic Income ÷ (Days Per Period ÷ 2) ÷ Hours Per Day. Example: 13,000 ÷ 13 ÷ 8 = 125.00.
- Payroll daily and hourly rates use the cutoff's working days (calendar dates minus the employee's timekeeping rest days). A 14-day cutoff is not divided by 13.
- Late, undertime, overtime, holiday, hours-worked, and dispute amounts use that same cutoff rate.

## 16:10 — Calendar-day payroll keeps full Basic Income

**Why:** When Use Payroll Calendar Days is on, absences should not shrink Basic Income. They are deductions, together with late and undertime.

**Files:**
- `app/Services/PayrollBatchService.php`
- `app/Support/PayrollCalendarWorkingDays.php`
- `resources/views/employees/partials/_employee-salary-panel.blade.php`
- `tests/Unit/PayrollCalendarWorkingDaysTest.php`

**Summary:**
- Basic Income on the payroll stays the full salary amount.
- Days in the cutoff with no attendance are an Absences deduction at Basic ÷ working days.
- Half-day absences, late, and undertime stay as deductions. Rest days are not absences.

## 16:16 — Rest days are not absences

**Why:** A rest day with no time in must not be posted as an Absences deduction.

**Files:**
- `app/Support/PayrollCalendarWorkingDays.php`
- `app/Services/PayrollBatchService.php`
- `app/Services/TimeLogsPayrollService.php`
- `app/Services/EmployeeLoadPayrollService.php`
- `resources/views/employees/partials/_employee-salary-panel.blade.php`
- `tests/Unit/PayrollCalendarWorkingDaysTest.php`

**Summary:**
- Absence days are the cutoff dates that are not rest days and have no attendance.
- Sunday (or any other rest day from the employee timekeeping setup) is left out even when there is no punch.
- A half-day absence on a rest day is not deducted.
