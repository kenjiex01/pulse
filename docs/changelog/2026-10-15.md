## 10:12 — Filter payroll batch employees by pay period category

**Why:** Add Employee on a payroll batch was listing every active employee with the same pay type, even when the pay period was scoped to one category.

**Files:**
- `app/Services/PayrollBatchService.php`
- `resources/views/payroll/transaction/_add-employees-form.blade.php`
- `tests/Unit/PayrollBatchEligibleEmployeesTest.php`

**Summary:**
- Eligible employees must have an employment record whose user type is one of the categories selected on the pay period, plus a matching pay-type salary for the period dates.
- A hybrid employee still appears when one of their roles matches the period.
- Periods with no category saved keep the previous pay-type-only list.
