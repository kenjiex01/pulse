## 11:13 — No real time out is absent; early out uses a 1-hour half-day buffer

**Why:** IN logs and punches a few minutes apart were saved as time out, so a day with no real out became undertime instead of absent.

**Files:**
- `app/Services/PayrollBreakService.php`
- `app/Services/TimeLogsPayrollService.php`
- `app/Services/FacultyLoadPayrollService.php`
- `app/Services/EmployeeAttendanceViewService.php`
- `app/Services/PayrollAttendanceHoursWorkedService.php`

- `app/Services/PayrollAttendanceLeaveService.php`
- `tests/Unit/TimeLogsIncompleteAndHalfDayOutTest.php`
- `tests/Unit/PayrollBreakServiceTest.php`

**Summary:**
- Another IN is not a time out. An OUT less than 5 minutes after IN is ignored. No real out → absent that day.
- A real early out on an 8-hour shift: under 5 hours worked is half-day absent. Shortfall below 4 hours is undertime (3 hours worked → half-day absent + 1 hour undertime). Five hours or more stays ordinary undertime.
