## 13:26 — Late over half a shift is half-day absent (not late)

**Why:** Pag more than half-day na ang late, hindi na dapat late deduction — half-day absent, at ang basic ay mula sa second half ng shift.

**Files:**
- `app/Services/TimeLogsPayrollService.php`
- `app/Services/EmployeeAttendanceViewService.php`
- `app/Services/PayrollAttendanceHoursWorkedService.php`
- `app/Services/PayrollAttendanceLeaveService.php`
- `app/Services/PayrollAttendanceDayBreakdownService.php`

- `app/Services/TimekeepingMemoAttendanceService.php`
- `app/Services/HolidayPayService.php`
- `tests/Unit/TimeLogsHalfDayLateTest.php` (new)

**Summary:**
- Late > kalahati ng scheduled work time (minus breaks) = **half-day absent**, hindi late. Walang LTDE.
- Sample: 2PM–11PM with 6–7PM break, in at 6:44PM → first half (2–6) absent; basic starts at **7PM** through shift end (11PM) = 4.00 hours.
- Daily-rate salaries count the day as 0.5; hourly / hours-worked / attendance view subtract the first-half hours from basic.
- If the employee clocks in after 7PM, basic starts at the actual time in (still no late).
- Leave path files half-day AWOL (not tardiness) when the policy has an AWOL leave type. Late breakdown skips the day.
