## 17:52 — Loan form balance includes payroll payments

**Why:** The loans list already subtracts payroll deductions, so the edit form Loan Balance must show the same remaining amount.

**Files:**
- `app/Models/EmployeeLoan.php`
- `app/Http/Controllers/EmployeeLoanController.php`
- `resources/views/employees/partials/_loan-form.blade.php`
- `resources/js/app.js`
- `tests/Unit/LoanPayrollServiceTest.php`

**Summary:**
- Edit Loan balance is principal minus paid from previous, deducted from new loan, and payroll payments already posted.
- Saving a loan also rejects a negative balance after those payroll payments.
- Example: 32,116.80 − 29,440.40 − 1,338.20 payroll payment = 1,338.20, matching the loans list.
