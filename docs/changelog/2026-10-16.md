## 09:16 — Loan interest is principal minus loan amount

**Why:** Loan Interest should be the gap between Principal Loan Amount and Loan Amount.

**Files:**
- `app/Models/EmployeeLoan.php`
- `app/Http/Controllers/EmployeeLoanController.php`
- `resources/views/employees/partials/_loan-form.blade.php`
- `resources/js/app.js`
- `tests/Unit/LoanPayrollServiceTest.php`

**Summary:**
- With a payment count, Principal Loan Amount stays amortization × no. of payments. 553.74 × 24 = 13,289.76. 553.74 × 12 = 6,644.88.
- Loan Interest fills in as principal − loan amount. 13,289.76 − 12,000 = 1,289.76.
- Loan Balance uses that principal, minus paid from previous and deducted from new loan.
